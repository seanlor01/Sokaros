SOKAROS started the way a lot of good games do: a group of friends messing around with ideas and seeing what we could build together. That turned into a habit of making games, and it hasn't stopped since.

I'm always on the lookout for the next new and exciting project, whether that's rolling a snowball through a whole town, flipping Space Invaders around so you're the alien, or holding off a virus swarm in a neon tower defense. If it sounds fun to make, I want to try it.

Pick a game, hit play, and stay tuned. There's always something new in the works._
